## Check Your Thinking
Q1. What type of economic moat is demonstrated when a software platform is so deeply embedded in a business that replacing it would cause massive operational disruption and cost?
A. Brand advertising moat
B. High switching costs
C. Unregulated monopoly
D. Negative working capital
Hint: It is painful and costly for the customer to switch to a competitor.
Correct Answer: B
Concept / Fact Explanation: High switching costs keep customers because the financial and operational friction of changing providers outweighs the benefit of switching.

Q2. What does a "Golden Cross" on a technical price chart indicate to analysts?
A. The company is distributing free physical gold
B. A short-term moving average (like the 50-day MA) crosses decisively above a long-term moving average (like the 200-day MA), signaling bullish momentum
C. The stock market is closing permanently
D. The stock has zero trading volume
Hint: A fast-moving trendline rises above a slow-moving baseline.
Correct Answer: B
Concept / Fact Explanation: A Golden Cross occurs when a short-term moving average crosses above a long-term moving average, which analysts read as a sign of bullish momentum.

Q3. According to SPIVA scorecards, roughly what share of actively managed Indian large-cap equity funds fail to beat their benchmark index over a 10-year horizon?
A. Exactly 0%
B. About three-quarters (76% in the year-end 2025 report)
C. 10%
D. None — 100% of active funds beat the index
Hint: The large majority of active large-cap managers underperform their benchmark over long periods.
Correct Answer: B
Concept / Fact Explanation: The SPIVA India year-end 2025 report found that 76.3% of active large-cap funds lagged their benchmark over 10 years (and 84.4% over 5 years), after fees and expenses.

Q4. Under the old tax regime, what exclusive additional tax deduction is available for contributions to the National Pension System (NPS) under Section 80CCD(1B)?
A. Up to ₹50,000 above the Section 80C limit
B. ₹5,00,000
C. ₹1,000
D. Zero deductions
Hint: It provides an extra ₹50,000 tax deduction specifically for NPS Tier-1 accounts.
Correct Answer: A
Concept / Fact Explanation: Under the old tax regime, Section 80CCD(1B) gives an exclusive deduction of up to ₹50,000 for NPS Tier-1 contributions, over and above the ₹1.5 lakh limit of Section 80C.

Q5. What minimum percentage of Net Distributable Cash Flows must Indian REITs distribute to unitholders under SEBI regulations?
A. Exactly 5%
B. At least 90%
C. 50%
D. Zero percent
Hint: SEBI mandates that the vast majority of rental income must be paid out to investors.
Correct Answer: B
Concept / Fact Explanation: SEBI regulations require REITs to distribute at least 90% of net distributable cash flows to unitholders at least once every six months.

Q6. What was the total cost of the extra 1.5% annual fee on a ₹10,000 monthly SIP over 20 years in our case study?
A. ₹100
B. Approximately ₹18.2 Lakh (₹99.9 lakh in the index fund vs ₹81.8 lakh in the active fund)
C. ₹500
D. ₹1 crore
Hint: The fee difference wiped out over ₹18 lakh of final wealth.
Correct Answer: B
Concept / Fact Explanation: Compounding an extra 1.5% fee over 20 years reduces the final amount from about ₹99.9 lakh (at 12%) to about ₹81.8 lakh (at 10.5%), a loss of about ₹18.2 lakh.

Q7. What does an RSI (Relative Strength Index) reading below 30 typically indicate about a stock's short-term momentum?
A. The stock is in an overbought condition
B. The stock is in an oversold condition, where heavy selling pressure may be exhausted
C. The company has stopped paying taxes
D. The stock has a P/E ratio of zero
Hint: RSI values below 30 reflect heavy selling and stretched downside momentum.
Correct Answer: B
Concept / Fact Explanation: An RSI below 30 signals an oversold state, suggesting that selling may be overdone and a technical rebound is possible.

Q8. Why do low-cost Index Funds charge much lower expense ratios (<0.2%) than actively managed funds (~1.5%)?
A. Index funds mechanically track a benchmark and do not need expensive teams of research analysts and fund managers
B. Index funds are sponsored by charitable trusts
C. Index funds only operate on weekends
D. Index funds do not own real shares
Hint: Passive replication removes expensive management overhead.
Correct Answer: A
Concept / Fact Explanation: Passive index funds automatically track a benchmark, removing the cost of research, forecasting, and active trading.

Q9. At retirement (age 60), what minimum portion of the NPS fund must be used to buy a regular monthly pension annuity?
A. 0%
B. At least 20% for most private-sector subscribers with a fund above ₹12 lakh (at least 40% for government employees)
C. 100%, with no lump-sum option
D. Exactly ₹500
Hint: Since December 2025, the minimum annuity for most non-government subscribers fell from 40% to 20%.
Correct Answer: B
Concept / Fact Explanation: Under PFRDA's December 2025 rules, non-government subscribers with a fund above ₹12 lakh must put at least 20% into an annuity (and can take up to 80% as a lump sum, of which 60% is tax-free); government employees must still put at least 40% into an annuity.

## Explore Further
Q1. What does the "Capital Asset Pricing Model" (CAPM) calculate for an equity security?
A. The expected return of an asset based on its systematic risk (β) relative to the market: E(Rᵢ) = R_f + βᵢ × [E(R_m) − R_f]
B. The physical construction cost of a factory
C. The annual dividend payout ratio
D. The maximum personal loan an investor can borrow
Hint: It links expected return directly to the risk-free rate and systematic beta risk.
Correct Answer: A
Fact / Concept Explanation: CAPM sets out a straight-line relationship between expected return and systematic risk (β): expected return equals the risk-free rate plus a risk premium.

Q2. What is "Convexity" in fixed-income and bond portfolio management?
A. The curvature of the price-yield relationship, measuring how duration changes as market interest rates shift
B. The total number of bond traders in an exchange
C. The penalty fee for paying taxes late
D. The physical shape of a paper bond certificate
Hint: It measures the non-linear curve in a bond price's sensitivity to interest rate moves.
Correct Answer: A
Fact / Concept Explanation: Convexity measures the second derivative of a bond's price with respect to interest rates, capturing the curvature of price-yield sensitivity.

Q3. In behavioral finance, what is "Recency Bias" and how does it harm investor portfolios?
A. The tendency to overweight recent market performance and assume recent trends will continue indefinitely, leading to buying at market peaks and selling at market bottoms
B. Forgetting your bank account number
C. The habit of checking company earnings once every decade
D. Buying only newly listed IPO shares
Hint: Investors extend recent good or bad news far into the future.
Correct Answer: A
Fact / Concept Explanation: Recency bias makes people project recent short-term market trends into the distant future, leading to poor timing and emotional trading.

Q4. What is the "Sortino Ratio" and how does it improve upon the traditional Sharpe Ratio?
A. It only penalizes downside (negative) volatility rather than total volatility, giving a clearer measure of risk for uneven return patterns
B. It measures the physical weight of cash reserves
C. It calculates the total number of employees in a company
D. It is used exclusively for evaluating gold bullion
Hint: It separates harmful downside volatility from welcome upside volatility.
Correct Answer: A
Fact / Concept Explanation: The Sortino ratio modifies the Sharpe ratio by dividing excess return by downside deviation only, so it penalizes only negative returns.

Q5. What is the ultimate objective of building an "All-Weather" diversified asset allocation across Equities, Debt, Gold, and Real Estate?
A. Guaranteeing that your portfolio doubles in value every single week
B. Combining asset classes that tend to do well in different economic conditions (growth, inflation, deflation, recession), to maximize long-term risk-adjusted compounding
C. Eliminating the need to pay for household groceries
D. Ensuring that you never have to use digital bank transfers
Hint: Assets that don't move together balance each other across different economic conditions.
Correct Answer: B
Fact / Concept Explanation: An all-weather allocation holds assets with low correlation that balance each other during inflation, recession and growth, smoothing the compounding curve.
