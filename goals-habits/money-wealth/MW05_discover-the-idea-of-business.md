# MW05 - Discover the Idea of Business
**Life Stage:** 5–7 — Early Explorer | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Entrepreneurship

## Goal
Discover the Idea of Business

**Variable:** Business ideas (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Pretend Lemonade Stand: Set up a play counter with signs and 'sell' 3 refreshments to family members.
2. The Bookmark Factory: Make 3 colorful handmade bookmarks and distribute them to family readers.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Play shop
Set up a 10-minute shop counter role-play with siblings or parents.

### Habit 2 - Make simple products
Draw, color, or construct 1 craft item specifically designed for someone else's use.

### Habit 3 - Pretend to sell
Describe the features and 'price' of an item clearly to a pretend customer.

### Habit 4 - Learn buyer/seller roles
Alternate between acting as the cashier and the shopper during playtime.

### Habit 5 - Identify problems to solve
Point out 1 disorganized corner in the playroom and suggest how to fix it.

## Recommended Learning
The Little Creator (Buyers, sellers, making useful items, fair trades)
