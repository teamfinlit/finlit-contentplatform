# MW07 - Learn That Experiences Can Be Valuable
**Life Stage:** 5–7 — Early Explorer | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Lifestyle & Experiences

## Goal
Learn That Experiences Can Be Valuable

**Variable:** Meaningful experiences (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Zero-Cost Adventure: Lead a 30-minute park/nature walk with zero purchases, finding 3 natural treasures.
2. Memory Postcard: Draw a picture of your favorite moment after an outing instead of buying a souvenir.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Choose family activities
Pick 1 free outdoor or park activity for the weekend family schedule.

### Habit 2 - Discuss experiences
Name the happiest experience or memory from your day during dinner.

### Habit 3 - Learn travel basics
Pack your own small outing backpack with a water bottle and sun hat.

### Habit 4 - Appreciate free activities
Enjoy an entire library visit or park trip without asking for a store purchase.

### Habit 5 - Avoid demanding purchases
Walk through a retail store aisle calmly without asking for items off the shelf.

## Recommended Learning
Memories Over Things (Intangible joy, free community/nature activities, savoring moments)
