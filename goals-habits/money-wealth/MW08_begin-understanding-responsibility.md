# MW08 - Begin Understanding Responsibility
**Life Stage:** 5–7 — Early Explorer | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Independence

## Goal
Begin Understanding Responsibility

**Variable:** Responsibilities completed (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Duty Chart Sprint: Complete 1 personal daily chore (e.g., making bed) for 7 straight days without reminders.
2. Consequence Cleanup: Independently clean up an accidental spill or mess calmly without fussing.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Complete simple chores
Make your own bed immediately upon waking up every morning.

### Habit 2 - Care for belongings
Hang up your clothes or place dirty laundry directly into the hamper.

### Habit 3 - Help with small tasks
Carry 1 safe, unbreakable grocery item or clear your plate after eating.

### Habit 4 - Track responsibilities
Mark a daily completion star on a wall or fridge chore chart.

### Habit 5 - Learn consequences of choices
Identify 1 positive outcome achieved by finishing a daily duty on time.

## Recommended Learning
My Actions, My Impact (Accountability, contributing to family team, choices and consequences)
