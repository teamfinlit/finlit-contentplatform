# MW33 - Choose a Strong Career Direction
**Life Stage:** 17–18 — Transition to Adulthood | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Career & Profession

## Goal
Choose a Strong Career Direction

**Variable:** Career clarity (Unit: Score / 10)
**Target Period:** 24 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Higher Education & Career ROI Model: Calculate total cost of attendance (tuition, housing) vs expected starting salary for 2 degree paths.
2. Professional Network Launch: Build an optimized LinkedIn profile, connect with 25 alumni in your target field, and conduct 2 interviews.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Research careers
Read industry outlook reports and market trends for your chosen specialty every fortnight.

### Habit 2 - Compare qualifications
Assess the market value and employer recognition of specialized certificates versus university degrees.

### Habit 3 - Explore industries
Track quarterly earnings and hiring trends across 2 major industries of interest.

### Habit 4 - Speak to professionals
Send 2 polite informational interview outreach messages monthly to practitioners in your field.

### Habit 5 - Create career plan
Update a rolling 1-year and 3-year professional roadmap with tangible skill and milestone targets.

## Recommended Learning
Strategic Career Mapping & Market Alignment (Vocational & degree pathways, higher ed ROI, networking)
