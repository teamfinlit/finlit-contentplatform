# MW23 - Understand How Wealth Grows
**Life Stage:** 11–13 — Early Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Investing

## Goal
Understand How Wealth Grows

**Variable:** Investment concepts (Unit: Concepts)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Paper Portfolio Simulator: Manage a $10,000 virtual paper portfolio across 3 diversified index funds/stocks for 30 days.
2. Rule of 72 Case Study: Compute doubling periods at 4%, 8%, and 12% returns, documenting the power of starting early.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn compounding
Calculate the compound future value of a regular investment using different growth rates.

### Habit 2 - Study simple investments
Research 1 publicly traded company or index ETF and summarize how it earns revenue.

### Habit 3 - Understand risk
Identify the primary downside risk factors of an asset before looking at its gains.

### Habit 4 - Use simulations
Check and rebalance your virtual investment simulator portfolio weekly.

### Habit 5 - Review monthly
Analyze monthly simulator performance and connect results to broader economic events.

## Recommended Learning
The Compounding Engine & Asset Classes (Stocks, ETFs, bonds, Rule of 72, time horizons, risk vs return)
