# MW18 - Build a Personal Saving System
**Life Stage:** 11–13 — Early Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Saving

## Goal
Build a Personal Saving System

**Variable:** Savings balance (Unit: ₹)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Sinking Fund Sprint: Run two distinct savings pots (1 emergency cushion, 1 gadget goal) and fund both fully.
2. Untouchable Buffer: Accumulate a baseline emergency fund ($50+) and keep it 100% intact for 60 consecutive days.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Track money received
Log every single cash inflow into a financial spreadsheet or app within 24 hours.

### Habit 2 - Save consistently
Automatically direct a minimum of 25% of any received funds straight to savings.

### Habit 3 - Set goals
Write out quantifiable 3-month and 6-month savings targets with target amounts.

### Habit 4 - Separate savings
Allocate incoming savings into designated envelopes or sub-accounts by goal.

### Habit 5 - Review monthly
Review monthly savings growth against your annual financial roadmap.
