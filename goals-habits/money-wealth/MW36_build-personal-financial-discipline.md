# MW36 - Build Personal Financial Discipline
**Life Stage:** 17–18 — Transition to Adulthood | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Saving

## Goal
Build Personal Financial Discipline

**Variable:** Savings rate (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 18 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The 90-Day Strict Budget Challenge: Adhere to a 50/30/20 budget for 3 consecutive months with zero unbudgeted debt or overdrafts.
2. The 40% Savings Rate Target: Achieve a 40%+ savings rate on all net earned income for 2 consecutive pay cycles.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Track income
Log all gross and net income immediately upon receipt into a centralized ledger.

### Habit 2 - Save first
Automate a direct deposit of at least 20% of net income into savings/investments on payday.

### Habit 3 - Budget monthly
Set line-item budget caps for rent, groceries, transport, and leisure before the start of each month.

### Habit 4 - Limit impulse purchases
Enforce a strict 14-day mandatory waiting period on any non-essential purchase over $50.

### Habit 5 - Review weekly
Spend 15 minutes every Sunday night auditing expenditures against category caps.

## Recommended Learning
Living Below Your Means & Cash Flow Control (50/30/20 budget framework, automated transfers, eliminating impulse buying)
