# MW03 - Learn Needs vs Wants
**Life Stage:** 5–7 — Early Explorer | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Spending

## Goal
Learn Needs vs Wants

**Variable:** Correct choices (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 6 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The 2-Basket Sort: Classify 10 kitchen pantry items by attaching green tags for Needs and yellow for Wants.
2. The Checkout Trade-off: Select two wanted treats at checkout, compare them, and put one back willingly.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Classify purchases
State whether a newly bought household item is a need or a want.

### Habit 2 - Compare choices
Choose between two snack brands based on quality and parental advice.

### Habit 3 - Discuss needs
Name 1 essential item needed for health and survival during family dinner.

### Habit 4 - Discuss wants
Share 1 fun want you desire and explain why life is okay without it.

### Habit 5 - Wait before asking to buy
Take 5 deep breaths and wait 1 minute before asking an adult to buy a toy in-store.

## Recommended Learning
Must-Haves vs Nice-to-Haves (Basic survival needs vs optional wants, prioritizing)
