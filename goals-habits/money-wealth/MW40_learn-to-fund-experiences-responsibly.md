# MW40 - Learn to Fund Experiences Responsibly
**Life Stage:** 17–18 — Transition to Adulthood | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Lifestyle & Experiences

## Goal
Learn to Fund Experiences Responsibly

**Variable:** Experience fund (Unit: ₹)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Self-Funded Expedition: Plan and execute a multi-day budget trip funded 100% through personal earnings, tracking expenses daily.
2. Zero-Debt Social Year: Attend all social milestones (prom, graduation trips, festivals) using pre-saved cash funds without consumer debt.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Set experience budget
Cap total entertainment and leisure spending to a strict percentage (e.g., 10%) of net income monthly.

### Habit 2 - Save beforehand
Establish dedicated sinking funds for planned social events and holidays months in advance.

### Habit 3 - Compare travel costs
Compare booking dates, transit options, and hostels to secure the most economical travel plan.

### Habit 4 - Avoid unplanned spending
Enforce a strict cash-only or pre-loaded debit card limit during weekend parties and social events.

### Habit 5 - Prioritize meaningful experiences
Evaluate social plans based on relational connection and personal growth rather than peer prestige.

## Recommended Learning
Intentional Travel & Social Balance (Budget backpacking, group coordination, avoiding debt-fueled status spending)
