# MW22 - Learn Personal Budget Management
**Life Stage:** 11–13 — Early Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Budgeting

## Goal
Learn Personal Budget Management

**Variable:** Budget adherence (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. 60-Day Zero-Based Run: Build and adhere to a zero-based budget for 2 straight months where every dollar has a job.
2. Variance Autopsy: At month-end, compare budgeted vs actual figures, calculating % variances and reallocating.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Create monthly budget
Draft next month's line-item budget allocations before the 1st of the month.

### Habit 2 - Record expenses
Enter daily expense amounts into their assigned budget categories each evening.

### Habit 3 - Categorize spending
Tag each transaction strictly as Needs, Wants, or Savings.

### Habit 4 - Review variance
Conduct a weekly 15-minute budget audit to catch overspending early.

### Habit 5 - Adjust next month
Adjust next month's allocations to compensate for any category deficits.

## Recommended Learning
Zero-Based & 50/30/20 Systems (Fixed vs variable expenses, category allocations, variance forensics)
