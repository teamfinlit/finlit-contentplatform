# MW09 - Build Strong Money Basics
**Life Stage:** 8–10 — Young Learner | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Literacy

## Goal
Build Strong Money Basics

**Variable:** Financial concepts mastered (Unit: Concepts)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Cashier Shadow: Calculate total cost of 3 shopping items, estimate change, and audit the printed receipt.
2. Unit Price Showdown: Compare 2 package sizes of the same product to determine the cheapest cost per gram/ml.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn money vocabulary
Learn and define 1 new financial word weekly (e.g., discount, receipt, balance).

### Habit 2 - Practice calculations
Mentally compute the change due from a transaction before paying.

### Habit 3 - Compare prices
Check price tags of two rival brands on the supermarket shelf before selecting.

### Habit 4 - Discuss financial choices
Ask a parent why they chose a specific payment method or store brand.

### Habit 5 - Review weekly
Spend 5 minutes every Sunday auditing your wallet balance and weekly log.

## Recommended Learning
Everyday Financial Math (Calculating change, discounts, unit prices, cash vs digital)
