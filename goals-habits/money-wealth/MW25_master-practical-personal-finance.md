# MW25 - Master Practical Personal Finance
**Life Stage:** 14–16 — Developing Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Literacy

## Goal
Master Practical Personal Finance

**Variable:** Topics mastered (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 18 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Personal Banking Simulator: Open or simulate a student checking account, maintaining zero overdraft fees for 60 straight days.
2. Financial Risk & Fraud Drill: Conduct an account security audit—enable 2FA and review 3 common teen phishing/crypto scams.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn budgeting
Allocate every incoming dollar into categorized digital envelopes (spend/save/give) at the start of each month.

### Habit 2 - Learn banking
Log into your banking app once a week to verify account balances and check for unrecognized transaction fees.

### Habit 3 - Learn interest
Calculate the effective monthly interest growth on a savings deposit using varying APY rates.

### Habit 4 - Learn taxes basics
Examine a sample pay stub and identify gross pay, net pay, FICA/income taxes, and pre-tax deductions.

### Habit 5 - Learn financial risk
Analyze 1 real-world financial fraud or high-risk scheme weekly and document the red flags.

## Recommended Learning
Applied Personal Finance (Checking/debit cards, banking apps, compounding, paystubs & taxes basics)
