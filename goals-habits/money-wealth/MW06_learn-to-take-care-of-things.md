# MW06 - Learn to Take Care of Things
**Life Stage:** 5–7 — Early Explorer | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Discipline

## Goal
Learn to Take Care of Things

**Variable:** Items cared for (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Toy Hospital Day: Inspect toys, clean 5 items, and fix 1 broken toy with tape/glue alongside an adult.
2. The Borrowed Item Return: Borrow a book/toy and return it in flawless condition exactly when promised.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Keep belongings safely
Place backpack, shoes, and jacket in their assigned places immediately upon coming home.

### Habit 2 - Repair before replacing
Show an adult any loose button or torn page right away so it can be mended.

### Habit 3 - Avoid waste
Serve only as much food on your plate as you can finish completely.

### Habit 4 - Organize possessions
Sort all toys and books neatly into their storage bins before bedtime.

### Habit 5 - Return borrowed items
Hand back a borrowed toy with a 'thank you' immediately after playing.

## Recommended Learning
Care, Repair, and Respect (Belongings preservation, replacement costs, respecting property)
