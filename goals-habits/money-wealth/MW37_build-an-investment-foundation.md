# MW37 - Build an Investment Foundation
**Life Stage:** 17–18 — Transition to Adulthood | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Investing

## Goal
Build an Investment Foundation

**Variable:** Investment knowledge (Unit: Score / 100)
**Target Period:** 18 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Tax-Advantaged Account Setup: Research Roth IRA / ISA / PPF accounts, understand limits, and simulate the initial allocation into a broad ETF.
2. 10-Year Asset Growth Model: Model a multi-asset allocation ($200/mo recurring) over 10 years factoring in inflation and expense ratios.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn asset classes
Read 1 chapter of an investment book or verified investment guide every week.

### Habit 2 - Study compounding
Calculate the projected future value of small daily spends if invested in an index fund for 25 years.

### Habit 3 - Understand risk
Review historical bear market drawdowns (2000, 2008, 2020) to understand psychological volatility tolerance.

### Habit 4 - Use paper portfolio
Maintain a virtual paper investment account and evaluate its risk metrics (Sharpe ratio, volatility) quarterly.

### Habit 5 - Review quarterly
Conduct a quarterly rebalancing check to ensure holdings remain aligned with your target asset allocation.

## Recommended Learning
Bogleheads & Index Investing Fundamentals (Broad-market index ETFs, Roth IRAs/tax-advantaged accounts, market cycles)
