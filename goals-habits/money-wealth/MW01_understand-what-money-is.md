# MW01 - Understand What Money Is
**Life Stage:** 5–7 — Early Explorer | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Literacy

## Goal
Understand What Money Is

**Variable:** Money concepts learned (Unit: Concepts)
**Target Period:** 6 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Coin Detective: Identify, name, and sort 5 real coins/notes at home from lowest to highest value.
2. Tag Hunter: Locate and read aloud price tags on 3 grocery items before placing them into the cart.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn coins & notes
Name the denomination of 1 coin or note shown to you daily.

### Habit 2 - Identify prices
Find and read the price tag on 1 everyday household or store item.

### Habit 3 - Play shop games
Complete a 10-minute pretend grocery checkout role-play using play money.

### Habit 4 - Ask money questions
Ask an adult why one item costs more than another during shopping.

### Habit 5 - Learn needs vs wants
Label 1 grocery basket item as an absolute need or an optional want.

## Recommended Learning
The Story of Money & Value (Coins, bills, price tags, trade vs. buying, finite value)
