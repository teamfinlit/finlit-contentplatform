# MW27 - Explore High-Potential Career Paths
**Life Stage:** 14–16 — Developing Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Career & Profession

## Goal
Explore High-Potential Career Paths

**Variable:** Careers researched (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 18 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Career Roadmap Deep-Dive: Research 3 career paths, mapping education required, entry vs. mid salaries, and day-to-day duties into a 2-page brief.
2. Informational Interview: Formulate 5 questions, interview a working professional in an industry of interest, and write a summary.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Research careers
Spend 30 minutes every week reading deep dives into high-growth industry roles and emerging careers.

### Habit 2 - Interview professionals
Identify and reach out to 1 industry professional or mentor each month to ask about their career trajectory.

### Habit 3 - Compare qualifications
Compare degree vs. certification requirements and associated costs for 2 prospective career options.

### Habit 4 - Study earning potential
Check real salary ranges and compensation trajectories for targeted professions using transparent job boards.

### Habit 5 - Identify strengths
Log 1 personal strength or technical aptitude demonstrated during academic or extracurricular projects each week.

## Recommended Learning
Career Exploration & Skill Mapping (High-growth industries, educational paths, salaries, mentorship, market demand)
