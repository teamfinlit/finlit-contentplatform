# MW34 - Build a Job-Ready Skill
**Life Stage:** 17–18 — Transition to Adulthood | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Earning Power

## Goal
Build a Job-Ready Skill

**Variable:** Skill proficiency (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 24 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Accredited Competency Acquisition: Complete a recognized professional certification course (e.g., AWS Cloud Practitioner, Financial Modeling).
2. Freelance / Internship Contract: Secure and deliver your first paid freelance project, part-time professional job, or formal internship role.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Practice daily
Spend 60 minutes every day engaged in focused, hands-on practice of your core technical skill.

### Habit 2 - Build portfolio
Add 1 case study detailing a solved problem, methodology, and outcome to your public portfolio monthly.

### Habit 3 - Complete projects
Set and hit strict self-imposed delivery deadlines for all independent technical projects.

### Habit 4 - Get certifications where useful
Study for and pass 1 modular technical exam or certification assessment every 6 months.

### Habit 5 - Seek real feedback
Share project code, design drafts, or writing samples with an industry senior for direct critique.

## Recommended Learning
Professional Execution & Industry Certifications (Industry tools, client communication, technical certificates, portfolio)
