# MW04 - Understand How People Earn Money
**Life Stage:** 5–7 — Early Explorer | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Earning

## Goal
Understand How People Earn Money

**Variable:** Occupations understood (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Community Helper Interview: Ask a local storekeeper or postal worker 3 questions about their daily work tools.
2. Workplace Safari: Spot and name 5 distinct working professionals on a single neighborhood outing.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn about jobs
Read or listen to a short story about a different career or profession each week.

### Habit 2 - Discuss work
Ask an adult at dinner: 'What was one helpful task you solved at work today?'

### Habit 3 - Visit workplaces
Quietly observe how staff work and serve customers during an errand run.

### Habit 4 - Ask adults about careers
Ask a relative why they chose their profession and what they enjoy about it.

### Habit 5 - Play earning games
Complete an age-appropriate helper task (e.g., setting placemats) to earn a pretend token.

## Recommended Learning
Helping Others to Earn (Linking effort and service to earning, community professions)
