# MW17 - Become Financially Literate
**Life Stage:** 11–13 — Early Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Literacy

## Goal
Become Financially Literate

**Variable:** Concepts mastered (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Interest Simulator Lab: Model growth of $500 over 5 yrs at 5% simple vs compound interest; graph difference.
2. Statement Forensics: Audit a sanitized bank or utility statement with an adult, flagging fees, taxes, and rates.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn financial vocabulary
Define 1 advanced financial term weekly (e.g., APR, inflation, liquidity).

### Habit 2 - Study real examples
Read 1 real-world business headline or economic story every week.

### Habit 3 - Calculate percentages
Compute exact percentage discounts and sales tax on purchases in real time.

### Habit 4 - Learn interest basics
Calculate the monthly interest return on your bank savings balance.

### Habit 5 - Review monthly
Dedicate the last Saturday of each month to reviewing all newly learned financial terms.

## Recommended Learning
Banking, Interest & Inflation (Simple vs compound interest, inflation erosion, banking fees, digital safety)
