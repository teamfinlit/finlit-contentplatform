# MW42 - Increase Earning Capacity
**Life Stage:** 19–22 — Emerging Adult | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Earning Power

## Goal
Increase Earning Capacity

**Variable:** Annual income (Unit: ₹)
**Target Period:** 36 months

## Goal Challenges
1. First Professional Compensation Negotiation: Benchmark your market compensation and practice a scripted negotiation for salary/benefits.
2. Secondary Income Stream Generation: Build an independent side income stream (freelance consulting, digital product) earning $500+/mo consistently.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Build high-value skills
Acquire or master 1 new high-demand technical tool or programming library every 6 months.

### Habit 2 - Monetize skills professionally
Pitch your specialized services to 2 prospective corporate or freelance clients every month.

### Habit 3 - Track market value
Audit current job postings quarterly to evaluate entry/mid-tier salary ranges for your skill set.

### Habit 4 - Build portfolio
Publish comprehensive case studies of your professional work online showcasing measurable business impact.

### Habit 5 - Seek higher-value opportunities
Apply to or interview for at least 1 career advancement role every 6 months to benchmark market demand.

## Recommended Learning
Compensation Negotiation & Income Stacking (Market salary research, performance raises, freelancing, consulting, skill arbitrage)
