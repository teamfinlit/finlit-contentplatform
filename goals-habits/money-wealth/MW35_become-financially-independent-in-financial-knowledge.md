# MW35 - Become Financially Independent in Financial Knowledge
**Life Stage:** 17–18 — Transition to Adulthood | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Literacy

## Goal
Become Financially Independent in Financial Knowledge

**Variable:** Topics mastered (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 18 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Mock Tax Return & Paystub Audit: Complete a comprehensive mock tax return based on sample income data, calculating deductions and tax.
2. Credit & Lending Master Analysis: Research credit mechanics and calculate the interest difference on a $15,000 loan between 620 and 760 scores.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn banking
Review your checking, savings, and credit account balances and statement fees every week.

### Habit 2 - Learn taxes
Read official IRS/tax authority guidance on standard deductions, taxable vs non-taxable income monthly.

### Habit 3 - Understand credit
Check your credit report from authorized bureaus once every 4 months to verify data accuracy.

### Habit 4 - Learn insurance basics
Learn and explain key insurance terms (premium, deductible, copay, out-of-pocket maximum) with sample policies.

### Habit 5 - Review finances monthly
Conduct a formal monthly financial review reconciling income, spending, tax reserves, and net savings.

## Recommended Learning
Adult Financial Infrastructure (Filing income tax returns, credit scores & bureaus, insurance fundamentals, bank loan structures)
