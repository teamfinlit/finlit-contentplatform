# MW28 - Build Job-Ready Skills
**Life Stage:** 14–16 — Developing Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Earning Power

## Goal
Build Job-Ready Skills

**Variable:** Employable skills (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 24 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Marketable Skill Portfolio: Learn a specialized digital/creative tool (e.g., Python, Figma, Premiere) and build 3 end-to-end portfolio projects.
2. Client Delivery Milestone: Offer your specialized skill to a real student club or local business and secure a signed testimonial.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Develop digital skill
Dedicate 45 minutes 4 times a week to deliberate practice of a marketable digital or technical skill.

### Habit 2 - Practice English/communication
Deliver 1 structured presentation or write 1 concise project proposal per month to hone clear articulation.

### Habit 3 - Build portfolio
Refine, document, and publish 1 new project or code/design asset to a public portfolio monthly.

### Habit 4 - Complete projects
Take an open-ended project from conception to 100% completion without leaving loose ends.

### Habit 5 - Seek feedback
Request structured critique on your work from an instructor, mentor, or online peer group every two weeks.

## Recommended Learning
Skill Monetization & Digital Leverage (High-leverage skills: coding, writing, video editing, public speaking, digital tools)
