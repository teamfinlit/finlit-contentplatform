# MW26 - Build Strong Financial Discipline
**Life Stage:** 14–16 — Developing Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Saving

## Goal
Build Strong Financial Discipline

**Variable:** Savings rate (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 18 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The 35% Savings Sprint: Maintain an audited savings rate of 35%+ across all allowance, gift, and part-time earnings for 3 consecutive months.
2. Emergency Cushion Target: Accumulate a liquid cash reserve equal to 3 months of typical personal expenses in a separate high-yield account.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Track all money
Record every single dollar earned and spent into an expense-tracking app within 24 hours.

### Habit 2 - Save first
Transfer a predetermined percentage directly to savings the exact day money is received before spending anything.

### Habit 3 - Set financial goals
Set and track milestone deadlines for 2 active goals: 1 short-term (<3 months) and 1 medium-term (>6 months).

### Habit 4 - Control discretionary spending
Set a weekly cap on discretionary purchases (cafes, gaming, snacks) and stop immediately upon hitting the limit.

### Habit 5 - Review monthly
Perform a monthly net savings audit to measure actual savings progress against planned targets.

Targeted Sinking Funds & Savings Velocity (Automating transfers, multi-tier savings targets, managing lifestyle creep)
