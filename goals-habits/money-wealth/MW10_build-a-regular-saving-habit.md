# MW10 - Build a Regular Saving Habit
**Life Stage:** 8–10 — Young Learner | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Saving

## Goal
Build a Regular Saving Habit

**Variable:** Savings rate (Unit: % of money received)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The 30% Pay-Yourself-First Sprint: Allocate at least 30% of all allowances/gifts into savings for 30 days.
2. Visual Milestone Bar: Create a milestone progress bar for a medium-term purchase and log 4 weekly deposits.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Save every allowance/gift
Move a fixed percentage of any received pocket money directly into savings on day one.

### Habit 2 - Track savings
Log current total accumulated savings in a notebook or tracking app once a week.

### Habit 3 - Set savings goals
Write down a specific target item, exact cost, and target completion date.

### Habit 4 - Compare progress
Compare actual savings against your monthly milestone target at month-end.

### Habit 5 - Practice delayed gratification
Wait a mandatory 7 days before purchasing any non-essential item.

## Recommended Learning
The Accumulation Engine (Pay-yourself-first rule, milestone tracking, fighting impulse spending)
