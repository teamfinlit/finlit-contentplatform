# MW43 - Master Adult Personal Finance
**Life Stage:** 19–22 — Emerging Adult | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Literacy

## Goal
Master Adult Personal Finance

**Variable:** Financial literacy score (Unit: /100)
**Target Period:** 24 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Complete Benefits Optimization Plan: Optimize an employee benefits package—maximize employer 401(k) match, HSA vs PPO, and tax withholdings.
2. Credit Score Optimization Sprint: Optimize credit utilization across cards to stay below 10% on statement dates for 6 straight months (740+ score).

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Understand taxes
Calculate effective tax rates and marginal tax brackets on projected earned income annually.

### Habit 2 - Understand insurance
Review health, dental, auto, and renter's insurance terms annually to verify coverage adequacy.

### Habit 3 - Learn credit
Pay off all credit card balances in full before the monthly due date to incur zero interest charges.

### Habit 4 - Study investing
Read 1 peer-reviewed financial paper or respected financial management book every quarter.

### Habit 5 - Review finances monthly
Perform a comprehensive monthly personal balance sheet and cash-flow audit on the last day of each month.

## Recommended Learning
Advanced Personal Financial Management (Tax brackets & deductions, employee benefits & 401k match, health insurance, credit optimization)
