# MW16 - Develop Respect for Money & Property
**Life Stage:** 8–10 — Young Learner | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Responsibility

## Goal
Develop Respect for Money & Property

**Variable:** Responsible actions (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. 7-Day Energy Audit: Monitor home utility habits, ensuring zero idle lights, taps, or phantom chargers.
2. DIY Maintenance Sprint: Clean and maintain your bicycle or sports kit thoroughly to extend its lifespan.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Avoid waste
Switch off lights, fans, and electronics immediately when leaving an empty room.

### Habit 2 - Care for possessions
Clean and store sports gear, books, and tools immediately after finishing use.

### Habit 3 - Return borrowed items
Return borrowed school stationery or books the morning after borrowing.

### Habit 4 - Repair where possible
Attempt a supervised fix on a torn strap or loose screw before asking for a replacement.

### Habit 5 - Discuss consequences
Calculate the replacement cost of an item that was damaged due to carelessness.

## Recommended Learning
Stewardship & Asset Care (Utility conservation, community property, repair vs waste, asset lifetime)
