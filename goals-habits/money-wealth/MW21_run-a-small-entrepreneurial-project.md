# MW21 - Run a Small Entrepreneurial Project
**Life Stage:** 11–13 — Early Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Entrepreneurship

## Goal
Run a Small Entrepreneurial Project

**Variable:** Project revenue/profit (Unit: ₹)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Micro-Venture Launch: Launch a service/product, pitch 10 prospects, secure 3 paying clients, and show net profit.
2. Profit Reinvestment Run: Calculate net margins from your project and reinvest 50%+ into scaling equipment/supplies.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Find a problem
Interview 1 prospective client weekly about a pain point they would pay to fix.

### Habit 2 - Develop solution
Build a rapid, functional prototype of your product or service checklist.

### Habit 3 - Price offering
Calculate unit economics ensuring at least a 30% gross profit margin.

### Habit 4 - Market ethically
Deliver a clear, honest pitch to 1 new potential customer every week.

### Habit 5 - Track revenue/cost
Record every single expense receipt and customer payment in a ledger.

## Recommended Learning
Lean Teen Startup (MVP creation, unit economics, pricing models, ethical sales, reinvestment)
