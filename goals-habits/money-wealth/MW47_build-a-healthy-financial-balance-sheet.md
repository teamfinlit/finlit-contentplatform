# MW47 - Build a Healthy Financial Balance Sheet
**Life Stage:** 19–22 — Emerging Adult | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Debt & Assets

## Goal
Build a Healthy Financial Balance Sheet

**Variable:** Net worth (Unit: ₹)
**Target Period:** 36 months

## Goal Challenges
1. High-Interest Debt Elimination Sprint: Implement the debt avalanche method to pay off 100% of any credit card or high-interest (>8%) debt.
2. Positive Net Worth Milestone: Track all assets and liabilities in a personal balance sheet, achieving positive net worth growth over 4 consecutive quarters.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Track assets/liabilities
Update a personal balance sheet monthly tracking cash, investments, debts, and total net worth.

### Habit 2 - Avoid unnecessary debt
Never take out financing or Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) loans for consumable or depreciating items.

### Habit 3 - Repay expensive debt
Direct all spare discretionary cash towards highest-interest debt balances until fully extinguished.

### Habit 4 - Build savings
Consistently grow liquid net savings month-over-month regardless of external economic noise.

### Habit 5 - Review net worth quarterly
Audit net worth changes quarterly, identifying whether growth stemmed from savings or asset appreciation.

## Recommended Learning
Debt Elimination & Net Worth Acceleration (Debt avalanche vs snowball, avoiding toxic debt, net worth tracking, asset-to-liability ratios)
