# MW31 - Learn to Evaluate Financial Choices
**Life Stage:** 14–16 — Developing Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Decision Making

## Goal
Learn to Evaluate Financial Choices

**Variable:** Decisions evaluated (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Opportunity Cost Decision Log: Before making any non-essential purchase >$30, document 2 alternative uses and evaluate utility.
2. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Audit: Calculate the 3-year TCO for a smartphone, console, or laptop, including accessories and fees.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Compare alternatives
Identify and evaluate at least 3 distinct vendor alternatives before making any purchase above $20.

### Habit 2 - Calculate total cost
Add shipping, subscription costs, taxes, and replacement parts to the sticker price of an item prior to purchase.

### Habit 3 - Consider opportunity cost
Calculate how much an impulsive spend would grow to in 10 years if invested at 8% before buying.

### Habit 4 - Research before buying
Read warranty policies, user reviews, and return conditions before buying electronics or gear.

### Habit 5 - Review mistakes
Maintain an honest "Regret Log" of past impulse buys to identify recurring emotional spending triggers.

## Recommended Learning
Rational Decision Making & Opportunity Cost (Opportunity cost calculations, total cost of ownership, emotional spending)
