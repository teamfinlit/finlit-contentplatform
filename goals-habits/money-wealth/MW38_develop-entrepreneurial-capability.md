# MW38 - Develop Entrepreneurial Capability
**Life Stage:** 17–18 — Transition to Adulthood | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Entrepreneurship

## Goal
Develop Entrepreneurial Capability

**Variable:** Business experiments (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 24 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Service-to-Product Business Run: Launch a micro-business (tutoring, web dev, reselling), acquiring 5+ paying customers and $300+ net profit.
2. Business Unit Economics Analysis: Create a complete income statement (P&L) for your venture detailing CAC, COGS, and profit margins.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Identify problems
Log 2 actionable consumer pain points or operational bottlenecks observed in local businesses weekly.

### Habit 2 - Test ideas
Run a rapid low-cost market test (landing page, survey, social poll) before launching a product.

### Habit 3 - Learn sales
Read 1 educational sales or client negotiation article weekly to improve client conversion skills.

### Habit 4 - Build small projects
Prototype a minimum viable product or streamlined service package within 7 days of an idea.

### Habit 5 - Track project metrics
Update a weekly spreadsheet tracking leads, customer conversion rate, and net profit margins.

## Recommended Learning
Micro-Business Launch & Unit Economics (Customer acquisition channels, value propositions, profit margins, operational funnels)
