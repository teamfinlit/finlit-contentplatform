# MW20 - Develop Skills That Increase Future Earning Power
**Life Stage:** 11–13 — Early Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Earning Power

## Goal
Develop Skills That Increase Future Earning Power

**Variable:** High-value skills (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 18 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Portfolio Artifact 1.0: Master a digital tool (e.g., Canva, Python, video editing) and build a finished project.
2. External Solution Delivery: Solve a real technical problem for a school club, small business, or family team.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn digital skills
Spend 45 minutes practicing coding, digital design, or media skills 3 times a week.

### Habit 2 - Improve communication
Draft a formal, structured email pitch or proposal once a month.

### Habit 3 - Practice problem solving
Break down and solve 1 logic, math, or business problem weekly.

### Habit 4 - Build projects
Commit 2 hours every weekend to building an item for your digital portfolio.

### Habit 5 - Seek feedback
Request detailed feedback from an adult, mentor, or peer on your completed work.

## Recommended Learning
High-Value Digital & Technical Skills (Scalable tech, digital design/code, problem decomposition, portfolios)
