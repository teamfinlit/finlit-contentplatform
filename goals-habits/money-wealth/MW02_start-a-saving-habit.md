# MW02 - Start a Saving Habit
**Life Stage:** 5–7 — Early Explorer | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Saving

## Goal
Start a Saving Habit

**Variable:** Amount saved (Unit: ₹)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Goal Jar Setup: Create a transparent savings jar with a drawing of a target toy, depositing your first coin.
2. The 3-Day Hold: When wanting an impulse checkout item, wait 72 hours before deciding to purchase.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Use a savings box
Drop every gift or earned coin directly into your designated savings jar immediately.

### Habit 2 - Save part of gifts
Put at least 1 coin/bill from every received gift straight into savings.

### Habit 3 - Count savings weekly
Empty the savings jar every Sunday, count the total coins, and record progress.

### Habit 4 - Set tiny savings goals
Draw a picture of a target reward costing under 5 coins and place it by your jar.

### Habit 5 - Delay one small purchase
Say 'I will wait until tomorrow' for one checkout candy or treat.

## Recommended Learning
Piggy Banks & Patience (Saving jars, delayed gratification, watching money accumulate)
