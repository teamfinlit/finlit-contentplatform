# MW29 - Build & Test a Small Business Idea
**Life Stage:** 14–16 — Developing Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Entrepreneurship

## Goal
Build & Test a Small Business Idea

**Variable:** Business experiments (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Customer Validation Sprint: Interview 15 prospective customers about a shared pain point, summarize findings, and design an MVP solution.
2. The Paid Proof-of-Concept: Launch your MVP, pitch to 10 qualified prospects, and achieve at least 3 paid customer transactions.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Identify customer problem
Note down 1 unmet need, inefficiency, or consumer complaint observed in your community weekly.

### Habit 2 - Build prototype
Spend 2 hours every weekend iterating on a physical or digital product prototype.

### Habit 3 - Test demand
Pitch your value proposition to 3 potential users before investing money in supplies.

### Habit 4 - Sell ethically
Explain pricing, terms, and capabilities transparently to potential clients with zero misleading claims.

### Habit 5 - Measure results
Reconcile project revenues, direct material costs, and net operating profit after every transaction batch.

## Recommended Learning
Lean Validation & MVP Testing (Problem identification, customer interviews, prototyping, pricing models, ethical sales)
