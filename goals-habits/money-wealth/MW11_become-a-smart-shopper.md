# MW11 - Become a Smart Shopper
**Life Stage:** 8–10 — Young Learner | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Spending

## Goal
Become a Smart Shopper

**Variable:** Good purchase decisions (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Quality Inspector: Compare two competing stationery/toy items across materials, warranty, and price.
2. The 7-Day Cooling List: Place desired items on a 7-day hold list and review if desire still exists after 1 week.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Compare prices
Check prices across 2 different stores or websites before requesting a purchase.

### Habit 2 - Check quality
Inspect materials, build quality, and seams of any item prior to checkout.

### Habit 3 - Separate need/want
Tick a 'Need vs Want' checklist item before adding discretionary items to cart.

### Habit 4 - Wait before buying
Apply a strict 48-hour wait before buying any non-school related item.

### Habit 5 - Review purchases
Evaluate an item bought 30 days ago: rate if it delivered true lasting value.

## Recommended Learning
Consumer Detective: Value & Quality (Durability, marketing tricks, online reviews, buyer's remorse)
