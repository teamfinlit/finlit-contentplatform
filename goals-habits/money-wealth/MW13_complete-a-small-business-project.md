# MW13 - Complete a Small Business Project
**Life Stage:** 8–10 — Young Learner | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Entrepreneurship

## Goal
Complete a Small Business Project

**Variable:** Project completed (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Micro Enterprise: Make and sell handmade crafts/treats, calculate raw costs vs revenue, and record net profit.
2. Customer Pitch & Feedback: Present your product politely to 3 potential customers, closing at least 1 sale.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Identify a problem
Write down 1 everyday hassle or inconvenience you noticed during the week.

### Habit 2 - Create a product/service
Spend 1 hour on the weekend building a prototype solution or service checklist.

### Habit 3 - Set a price
Sum material costs and add a fair markup percentage to establish a sales price.

### Habit 4 - Sell ethically
Pitch your product honestly without misrepresenting features or benefits.

### Habit 5 - Calculate result
Calculate total revenue minus total expenses to find your final net earnings.

## Recommended Learning
Junior Founder Playbook (COGS, pricing, ethical sales, profit margins, customer communication)
