# MW19 - Develop Smart Spending Habits
**Life Stage:** 11–13 — Early Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Spending

## Goal
Develop Smart Spending Habits

**Variable:** Unnecessary purchases avoided (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The 30-Day Purchase Freeze: Put a non-essential purchase over $25 on a 30-day waitlist; re-evaluate desire.
2. Comparative Feature Teardown: Score 3 competing tech/apparel items on price, durability, and negative reviews.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Compare alternatives
Identify at least 2 alternative products before buying anything over $10.

### Habit 2 - Delay purchases
Mandate a 48-hour pause on micro-transactions and impulse online buys.

### Habit 3 - Research products
Read at least 3 critical/1-star reviews to detect potential product flaws.

### Habit 4 - Track spending
Record all daily out-of-pocket transactions in an expense log nightly.

### Habit 5 - Review mistakes
Analyze 1 past regretful purchase and identify the emotional trigger behind it.

## Recommended Learning
Marketing Manipulation & FOMO (Targeted ads, peer pressure, total cost of ownership, the 30-day delay)
