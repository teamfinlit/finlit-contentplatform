# MW41 - Build a Strong Career Foundation
**Life Stage:** 19–22 — Emerging Adult | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Career & Profession

## Goal
Build a Strong Career Foundation

**Variable:** Career progress (Unit: Milestones)
**Target Period:** 36 months

## Goal Challenges
1. High-Impact Internship / Apprenticeship: Secure and complete a competitive internship, presenting a tangible capstone project to leadership.
2. Professional Network Architecture: Build an active professional network of 100+ industry practitioners, securing 2 senior references.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Build expertise
Devote 1 hour daily to advancing technical domain knowledge beyond standard academic/job duties.

### Habit 2 - Complete internships/projects
Deliver every professional deliverable 24 hours ahead of deadline with zero unaddressed requirements.

### Habit 3 - Develop network
Attend 1 industry conference, webinar, or networking meetup monthly to expand professional contacts.

### Habit 4 - Seek mentors
Schedule quarterly 1-on-1 check-ins with an experienced industry professional for strategic guidance.

### Habit 5 - Review career quarterly
Evaluate professional achievements, resume updates, and skill gaps at the end of every quarter.

## Recommended Learning
Corporate Navigation & Professional Capital (Internship conversion, performance reviews, building human capital, mentorship)
