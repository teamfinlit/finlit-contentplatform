# MW32 - Plan Lifestyle Within a Budget
**Life Stage:** 14–16 — Developing Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Lifestyle & Experiences

## Goal
Plan Lifestyle Within a Budget

**Variable:** Planned budgets (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Teen Social Outing Producer: Organize a full-day group activity for 4 friends, ensuring total per-person cost stays strictly under $15.
2. The Subscription & Entertainment Cap: Perform an audit of entertainment and subscriptions, establishing a hard monthly ceiling.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Set spending limits
Establish a firm cash/card spending ceiling prior to entering any mall, entertainment venue, or event.

### Habit 2 - Research costs
Check travel, parking, food, and admission prices before agreeing to an outing with friends.

### Habit 3 - Save for experiences
Fund a planned trip or festival ticket 100% in advance through dedicated savings before purchasing.

### Habit 4 - Compare options
Propose 1 low-cost or free social alternative whenever an expensive group activity is suggested.

### Habit 5 - Review outcomes
Rate social outings after completion on a value-for-money scale to refine future recreation choices.

## Recommended Learning
Lifestyle Design & Social Cost Management (Peer spending pressure, cost-per-hour recreation, intentional lifestyle budgeting)
