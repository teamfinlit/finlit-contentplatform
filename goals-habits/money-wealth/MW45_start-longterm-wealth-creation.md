# MW45 - Start Long-Term Wealth Creation
**Life Stage:** 19–22 — Emerging Adult | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Investing

## Goal
Start Long-Term Wealth Creation

**Variable:** Invested amount (Unit: ₹)
**Target Period:** 36 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Automated DCA System: Set up an automated recurring monthly investment into a low-cost index ETF, maintaining it uninterrupted for 6 months.
2. Tax-Advantaged Maximization: Fully fund or execute a formal plan to reach the annual contribution ceiling for a tax-advantaged account (e.g., Roth IRA).

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Invest regularly
Direct a fixed percentage (minimum 15%) of every paycheck directly into long-term investments.

### Habit 2 - Learn diversification
Verify that your portfolio is diversified across thousands of underlying companies via index funds.

### Habit 3 - Understand risk
Adopt a minimum 7-to-10 year time horizon for all equity investments to withstand market cycles.

### Habit 4 - Avoid speculative decisions
Refuse to invest capital into hype assets, meme coins, or unverified tip-based trading schemes.

### Habit 5 - Review quarterly
Review portfolio allocation quarterly to ensure asset weights remain within target rebalancing bands.

## Recommended Learning
Systematic Investing & Modern Portfolio Theory (Tax-advantaged compounding (Roth IRA, 401k), low-cost index ETFs, Dollar-Cost Averaging)
