# MW46 - Test Entrepreneurial Potential
**Life Stage:** 19–22 — Emerging Adult | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Entrepreneurship

## Goal
Test Entrepreneurial Potential

**Variable:** Business experiments (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 36 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Minimum Viable Product Launch: Build, launch, and market a product, achieving at least $1,000 in gross revenue from genuine non-friend customers.
2. Customer Cohort Retention Audit: Run a structured feedback loop with your first 20 paying customers, measuring NPS and implementing iterations.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Solve real problems
Identify and document 1 commercially viable business problem or process inefficiency every week.

### Habit 2 - Interview customers
Conduct 2 customer discovery calls or interviews monthly with targeted buyers in your niche.

### Habit 3 - Test ideas
Test new product concepts using low-budget landing page experiments before full development.

### Habit 4 - Build sales
Dedicate 3 hours weekly to active outreach, sales calls, or marketing distribution channels.

### Habit 5 - Measure net profit
Reconcile monthly revenue, operational overhead, and net margin, keeping operating expenses lean.

## Recommended Learning
Scalable Ventures & Product-Market Fit (Validation sprints, MVP launch, B2B/B2C models, acquisition funnels, unit economics)
