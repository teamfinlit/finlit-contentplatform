# MW24 - Learn to Balance Spending & Experiences
**Life Stage:** 11–13 — Early Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Lifestyle & Experiences

## Goal
Learn to Balance Spending & Experiences

**Variable:** Planned experiences (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Event Producer: Plan, budget, and host an entertaining gathering/outing for peers under an all-inclusive $20 cap.
2. The Opportunity Cost Trade-Off: Skip 1 discretionary impulse spend and redirect the exact funds to an experience bucket.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Set experience budget
Pre-allocate a strict percentage cap for recreational activities every month.

### Habit 2 - Save beforehand
Save 100% of an event or trip cost in advance before booking or attending.

### Habit 3 - Compare alternatives
Identify 2 budget-friendly alternatives for every social outing suggestion.

### Habit 4 - Prioritize meaningful activities
Choose activities focused on skill acquisition or active connection over passive consumption.

### Habit 5 - Avoid impulsive spending
Enforce a strict zero-unplanned-spend rule while hanging out with friends.

## Recommended Learning
Intentional Living & Opportunity Cost (Cost-per-memory, group event budgeting, resisting social comparison)
