# MW39 - Make Responsible Major Financial Decisions
**Life Stage:** 17–18 — Transition to Adulthood | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Decision Making

## Goal
Make Responsible Major Financial Decisions

**Variable:** Decisions researched (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Higher Education Debt-to-Income Audit: Evaluate college options, capping expected student loan payments under 10% of projected gross salary.
2. First Apartment / Dorm Lease Forensics: Analyze a residential rental lease agreement, identifying deposit rules, fees, and renter insurance.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Compare options
Construct a weighted comparison matrix scoring at least 3 alternatives before any decision over $100.

### Habit 2 - Calculate costs
Compute financing fees, interest, maintenance, and insurance for major assets before buying.

### Habit 3 - Assess risk
Draft a worst-case scenario plan (loss of job, broken equipment) for any major financial commitment.

### Habit 4 - Seek advice
Consult a financially savvy mentor or advisor before signing contracts with multi-month obligations.

### Habit 5 - Document decisions
Write a 1-page decision memo documenting the financial logic, assumptions, and alternatives before major buys.

## Recommended Learning
High-Stakes Decision Frameworks (Student loan debt evaluation, vehicle purchase vs transit, lease contracts, expected value)
