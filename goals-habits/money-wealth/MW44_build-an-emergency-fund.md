# MW44 - Build an Emergency Fund
**Life Stage:** 19–22 — Emerging Adult | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Saving

## Goal
Build an Emergency Fund

**Variable:** Emergency fund (Unit: Months of expenses)
**Target Period:** 24 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Full 3-Month Emergency Fund: Calculate essential baseline living expenses and save 100% of a 3-month cash buffer in a separate HYSA.
2. Emergency Account Isolation: Lock emergency reserves into a dedicated institution separate from checking, with zero non-emergency withdrawals for 6 months.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Automate saving
Schedule automated direct deposits to the emergency savings account immediately upon payroll receipt.

### Habit 2 - Control lifestyle inflation
Keep fixed living expenses constant for at least 6 months after receiving any salary increase or bonus.

### Habit 3 - Track expenses
Log all living expenses into budgeting software to maintain an up-to-date monthly baseline figure.

### Habit 4 - Maintain cash reserve
Verify monthly that liquid savings meet or exceed the target 3-to-6 month essential expense threshold.

### Habit 5 - Review quarterly
Recalculate your emergency fund target whenever rent, utilities, or insurance rates change.

## Recommended Learning
Emergency Reserves & Liquid Capital Preservation (3-to-6 month expense buffer, High-Yield Savings Accounts, capital preservation)
