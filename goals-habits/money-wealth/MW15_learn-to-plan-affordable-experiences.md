# MW15 - Learn to Plan Affordable Experiences
**Life Stage:** 8–10 — Young Learner | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Lifestyle & Experiences

## Goal
Learn to Plan Affordable Experiences

**Variable:** Experiences planned (Unit: No.)
**Target Period:** 12 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The Budget Day Out: Plan a complete weekend outing for two under a fixed spending cap, covering entry and food.
2. Comparative Outing Matrix: Research 2 activities (e.g., museum vs movie) and evaluate cost per hour of fun.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Research costs
Look up entry tickets, parking, and transit fares online before any outing.

### Habit 2 - Set a budget
Agree on a strict maximum spending limit with family before leaving home.

### Habit 3 - Compare options
Identify at least 1 free or low-cost alternative before picking a paid leisure activity.

### Habit 4 - Save for experiences
Set aside a fixed portion of weekly savings into a dedicated 'Experience Jar'.

### Habit 5 - Reflect afterward
Rate the outing from 1–10 and evaluate whether the experience justified the spend.

## Recommended Learning
Smart Outing Architect (Cost research, community discounts, packing ahead, value-per-hour)
