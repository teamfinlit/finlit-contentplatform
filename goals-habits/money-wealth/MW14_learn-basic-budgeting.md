# MW14 - Learn Basic Budgeting
**Life Stage:** 8–10 — Young Learner | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Financial Discipline

## Goal
Learn Basic Budgeting

**Variable:** Budget accuracy (Unit: %)
**Target Period:** 6 months

## Goal Challenges
1. The 3-Envelope Challenge: Split monthly allowance into Spend (50%), Save (40%), and Give (10%) envelopes.
2. Budget vs Actual Audit: Project spending for an upcoming week, track actual spends, and compute variance.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Plan spending
Write down planned expenditures for the upcoming week every Sunday evening.

### Habit 2 - Record spending
Log every single cash or credit expense on the exact day it occurs.

### Habit 3 - Compare budget vs actual
Check if actual expenses stayed within planned category envelopes weekly.

### Habit 4 - Review weekly
Spend 10 minutes every Friday reconciling physical envelope cash against logs.

### Habit 5 - Adjust choices
Stop non-essential spending immediately once a specific envelope is empty.

## Recommended Learning
Envelope Budgeting & Variance (Give/Save/Spend buckets, expense tracking, staying within caps)
