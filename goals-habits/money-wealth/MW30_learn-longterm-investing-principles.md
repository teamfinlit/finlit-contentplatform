# MW30 - Learn Long-Term Investing Principles
**Life Stage:** 14–16 — Developing Teen | **Pillar:** Money & Wealth | **Sub-domain:** Investing

## Goal
Learn Long-Term Investing Principles

**Variable:** Investment concepts (Unit: Concepts)
**Target Period:** 18 months

## Goal Challenges
1. Index Investing Blueprint: Research broad-market index funds; write an analysis comparing index funds to actively managed funds over 20 years.
2. Paper Portfolio Stress Test: Construct a virtual $25,000 diversified paper portfolio (60/30/10) and backtest behavior during bear markets.

## Habits

### Habit 1 - Learn compounding
Simulate a $100/mo recurring contribution over 10, 20, and 30 years at 7% real returns using an investment calculator.

### Habit 2 - Study asset classes
Read 1 educational breakdown per week comparing risk and reward profiles across asset classes (stocks, bonds, real estate).

### Habit 3 - Learn diversification
Audit a sample portfolio to check sector weighting and verify no single company exceeds 10% of total holdings.

### Habit 4 - Understand risk
Identify the historical maximum drawdown for an asset class before evaluating its return track record.

### Habit 5 - Use simulations
Log into a paper-trading simulation weekly to rebalance target allocations rather than chasing top gainers.

## Recommended Learning
Asset Allocation & Long-Term Compounding (Index funds, ETFs, broad asset classes, dollar-cost averaging, market cycles)
